## Product Purpose

A personal profile for Muhammad Hidayat, a Fullstack Web Developer in Makassar, presented as a Koda Camp exercise. The visitor can read his introduction, hobbies, education, and follow his Instagram link. A hidden playable sword-fighting game is part of the experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in `level-1/task-1/`; preserve this zero-build setup.
- Preserve the supplied biography, Indonesian language, education, address, Instagram destination, and Koda Camp association.
- Reuse the supplied Soldier and Orc sprites and Fantasy RPG audio.
- The user requested better gameplay and whole-page design, explicitly delegating creative direction with “Surprise me, give me your best.”
- Support keyboard and touch interaction, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing page supplies personal details and education. Local sprite sheets and music are available in the repository. There are no supplied client projects, testimonials, or professional performance claims.
